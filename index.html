What to check before you order

Match the exact listing

Save the seller link, selected color and size. Compare the seller’s measurements with an item you already own. Measure both garments flat when comparing chest or shoulder width.

Review warehouse QC photos

Look for photos of your specific item. Match the size label and color to the order, then check seams and closures. Request a close-up or a ruler photo if a detail is unclear.

Compare delivered costs

Calculate merchandise, domestic delivery, agent fees, international shipping and applicable import charges in one currency. For example, $40 + $3 + $4 + $28 = $75 before import charges.

Frequently asked questions

What is a KakoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product discovery list that helps shoppers compare listings before using a buying agent. This independent guide features listings from CAFANS.

Are the catalog photos warehouse QC photos?

The photos shown here come from the catalog. Ask your agent for warehouse photos of your selected item and compare the color, size label and measurements before approving shipment.

Why might the shipping quote change after packing?

A carrier can charge by actual parcel weight or by the space the parcel occupies. Compare quotes for the same destination, packed dimensions and service level, and ask which fees the quote includes.
